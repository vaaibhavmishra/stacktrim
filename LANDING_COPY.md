## FAQ

### Is this free?

Yes. The audit is free and does not require login. Email is requested only after the report is shown.

### Do you use AI to calculate the savings?

No. Savings math is rule-based and cites official pricing pages. AI is used only to turn the result into a short personalized summary.

### What happens to my email?

Your email is stored separately from the public report. Public audit URLs show tools and savings, not contact details.

### Why does Credex appear on high-savings reports?

Large savings often involve credits, procurement leverage, or vendor consolidation. Credex can help convert the recommendation into real cash impact.

### What if the audit finds no savings?

The report says so. The tool should build trust, not invent fake cuts.
