# Economics

Assume Credex sells or brokers AI infrastructure credits and advisory packages. A converted lead is worth the gross margin on a credit purchase or consultation-driven procurement engagement. A conservative estimate: a high-savings startup buys or redirects $25,000 of credits, Credex earns 8% gross margin or equivalent advisory value, so one converted credit customer is worth $2,000 gross profit. If Credex also sells a $3,000 advisory package, blended first-order value could be $2,000-$5,000. I will model $2,500 gross profit per converted customer.

Channel CAC is mostly time at MVP stage. Hacker News and X launch posts have $0 cash CAC but high variance; assume 20 hours of founder time for 100 audits. At a $75/hour opportunity cost, that is $1,500 or $15 per completed audit. Direct founder DMs might take 10 hours for 30 audits, or $25 per audit. Community replies are cheaper, maybe $8-$12 per audit, but require credibility and useful examples.

Profitability math: 100 completed audits at $15 CAC each costs $1,500 in time. If 30% capture email, 10% of captured leads book a Credex consultation, and 30% of consultations convert to a credit purchase, then 100 audits produce 30 emails, 3 calls, and 0.9 customers. At $2,500 gross profit per customer, expected gross profit is $2,250, so the channel is profitable before product hosting costs. The break-even conversion from audit completed to customer is $15 / $2,500 = 0.6%. If consultation booking is 3%, the close rate from booked call to credit purchase only needs to be 20%.

For $1M ARR in 18 months, Credex needs this tool to become a repeatable lead engine rather than a launch spike. If ARR means advisory and credit-management revenue, assume $12,000 annual gross revenue per retained customer. That requires about 84 retained customers. At a 1% audit-to-customer conversion rate, the tool needs 8,400 qualified completed audits over 18 months, or roughly 467 per month. At 2%, it needs 234 per month. That is plausible only if public result URLs and benchmark content create compounding distribution, and if Credex follows up quickly on high-savings audits with concrete credit options.
